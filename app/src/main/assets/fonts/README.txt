Fonts used by the app (exact names, .ttf):

  Inter-Regular.ttf               English, normal text  (Inter 18pt, Latin-only subset, ~50 KB)
  Inter-Bold.ttf                  English, titles and buttons  (same, Bold)
  NotoSerifBengali-Regular.ttf    Bengali letters, normal text; chained behind Inter-Regular
  NotoSerifBengali-Bold.ttf       Bengali letters, titles and buttons; chained behind Inter-Bold
  OFL-Inter.txt                   Inter license (SIL OFL)
  OFL-NotoSerifBengali.txt        Noto Serif Bengali license (SIL OFL)

The Inter files are trimmed to Latin letters, digits and punctuation to keep the APK small.
Bengali letters (and the danda) come from Noto Serif Bengali. Other scripts come from system fonts.
