PDF Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Summary

PDF Manager has no server of its own. It has no analytics, no ads and no accounts. Your files stay on your device, except when you choose to convert a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file to PDF, or when you tap Send in Ask AI.

Google user data

For Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF, the app asks you to sign in with Google and grant one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets the app see and manage only the files it creates itself in your Google Drive. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.

  • When you tap Convert, the selected file is uploaded over an encrypted connection to your own Google Drive as a temporary file with a generic name.
  • Google converts it to PDF, the app downloads the PDF to your device, and the temporary Drive file is permanently deleted right away. If deletion fails, the app retries later.
  • The access token is kept in memory only and is never saved.
  • The app does not store your email address or Google account details.
  • The app does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Ask AI (Google Gemini)

Ask AI is optional and works only with your own Google Gemini API key, which you paste in Settings. Nothing is sent until you accept the first send dialog, and never in the background.

  • When you tap Send, the selected PDF content (this page, the pages you chose, or the whole PDF) and your question are sent over an encrypted connection to Google Gemini at generativelanguage.googleapis.com. For a large PDF the file is uploaded to Google and kept there for up to 48 hours.
  • If you use a free tier key, Google may use the content you send to improve its products. Check the terms that apply to your key.
  • Your API key is stored only in the app's private storage. It is excluded from Android backup, device transfer and the Google Drive backup, and it is never written to a log or a URL.
  • The conversation is kept in memory while the viewer is open and is not saved afterwards. Temporary page copies are deleted after each question.
  • Settings, AI, Clear AI data removes your key, choices and saved file links from the device.

Data kept on your device

The app keeps temporary files (for example, a converted PDF or page previews) and an optional text index for searching inside your PDFs, in its own private storage. You can turn off the index in the app, and everything is removed when you clear the app data or uninstall it. Your original files are never changed.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can remove the app's Google permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app also has a "Remove leftover temp files" option in Settings.
